Business Requirements & Process Management System

## Purpose

The Requirement Traceability Matrix connects business requirements with functional requirements, user stories, use cases, and acceptance criteria. It helps ensure that important requirements are properly covered throughout the project.

## Traceability Benefits

The RTM helps to:

- Ensure important business requirements are covered.
- Connect business needs with system functionality.
- Identify missing requirements.
- Support testing and validation.
- Track requirements throughout the development process.
- Reduce the possibility of requirements being missed.
