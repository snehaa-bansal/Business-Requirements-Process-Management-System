# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project
Business Requirements & Process Management System

## 1. What is a Stakeholder?

A stakeholder is a person or group that uses the system, provides requirements, is affected by the process, or has an interest in the outcome of the project.

## 2. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Employee | Primary User | Submit leave requests and track request status |
| Manager | Approver | Review and approve/reject employee requests |
| HR | Process Owner | Maintain leave records and generate reports |
| Management | Business Stakeholder | Monitor leave trends and workforce availability |
| Development Team | Technical Stakeholder | Understand requirements and develop the solution |

## 3. Stakeholder Details

### Employee

The employee is the primary user of the system. Employees need to submit leave requests, view their leave information, and track the status of submitted requests.

### Manager

The manager reviews leave requests submitted by employees and decides whether to approve or reject them.

### HR

HR is responsible for maintaining employee leave records, monitoring leave activity, and preparing leave-related reports.

### Management

Management requires summarized leave information to understand workforce availability and leave trends.

### Development Team

The development team uses the documented business and functional requirements to understand what the proposed system should do and implement the solution accordingly.
