# Business Problem Statement

## Project
Business Requirements & Process Management System

## Business Area
Human Resources – Employee Leave Management

## Organization
TechNova Solutions

## 1. Current Situation

TechNova Solutions currently manages employee leave requests through email communication and manually maintained spreadsheets.

Employees submit leave requests to their managers through email. Managers review the requests and approve or reject them. HR then manually updates the employee leave records in spreadsheets.

## 2. Problems Identified

The current leave management process creates several challenges:

1. Leave requests may be delayed because the process depends on email communication.
2. HR spends significant time manually maintaining leave records.
3. Employees do not have a centralized way to track their leave requests.
4. Manual data entry can result in incorrect or inconsistent records.
5. Managers have limited visibility into team leave information.
6. Preparing leave-related reports requires additional manual effort.

## 3. Business Need

The organization needs a centralized leave management process that allows employees to submit leave requests, managers to review and approve or reject requests, and HR to maintain accurate leave records.

## 4. Business Objective

The objective is to design a structured and centralized leave management solution that:

- Reduces manual work.
- Improves visibility of leave requests.
- Maintains accurate leave records.
- Provides employees with request status visibility.
- Makes leave information easier for HR and management to monitor.
- Improves the overall efficiency of the leave approval process.

## 5. Expected Business Outcome

The proposed solution should provide a clear and trackable leave workflow, reduce dependency on manual email and spreadsheet updates, improve access to leave information, and support better reporting and decision-making.
