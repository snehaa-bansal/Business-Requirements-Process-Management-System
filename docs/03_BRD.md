# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Name
Business Requirements & Process Management System

### Business Area
Human Resources – Employee Leave Management

### Organization
TechNova Solutions

## 2. Business Problem

The current employee leave process depends on email communication and manually maintained spreadsheets. This creates delays, additional administrative work, limited visibility, and a possibility of data-entry errors.

## 3. Business Objective

The organization wants to introduce a centralized leave management process that reduces manual work, improves visibility, and maintains accurate leave records.

## 4. Business Requirements

### BR-01 – Centralized Leave Management

The organization needs a centralized system for managing employee leave requests and records.

### BR-02 – Employee Leave Submission

Employees should be able to submit leave requests through the system.

### BR-03 – Leave Approval

Managers should be able to review and approve or reject employee leave requests.

### BR-04 – Leave Status Tracking

Employees should be able to view the status of their submitted leave requests.

### BR-05 – HR Record Management

HR should be able to view and maintain employee leave records.

### BR-06 – Leave Reporting

The system should provide leave-related information that can support HR and management reporting.

### BR-07 – Improved Process Visibility

The system should provide relevant users with clear visibility into leave requests and their current status.

## 5. Expected Business Benefits

- Reduced manual administrative work.
- Improved visibility of leave requests.
- More organized leave records.
- Reduced dependency on email communication.
- Easier monitoring and reporting.
- Improved employee and manager experience.

## 6. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Employee leave request submission.
- Manager approval/rejection.
- Leave request status tracking.
- HR leave record management.
- Basic leave reporting.
- User access based on role.

### Out of Scope

- Payroll processing.
- Employee salary management.
- Recruitment management.
- Performance management.
- Attendance biometric integration.
